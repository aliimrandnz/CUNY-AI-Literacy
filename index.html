About the Initiative

The CUNY AI Literacy and Professional Readiness Series is a Spring 2026 initiative focused on helping students develop practical and responsible AI skills. The program brings together students from across CUNY and NYU through hands-on, industry-informed sessions that connect classroom learning with real-world applications in research, business, and career development.

Supported by a grant from the CUNY AI Innovation Fund through the Office of Academic Innovation, Brooklyn College has launched the CUNY AI Literacy & Professional Readiness Series, a new initiative featuring industry-led lectures and learning opportunities designed to equip CUNY students with applied, ethical AI skills directly tied to career readiness and the future of work. Participants in the series earn a digital badge as part of a micro-credential pathway designed to help students demonstrate AI readiness.

Led by Dr. Ngoc Cindy Pham, associate professor in the Murray Koppelman School of Business at Brooklyn College, the series is part of CUNY’s broader efforts in AI leadership development and workforce-focused AI literacy programming.
