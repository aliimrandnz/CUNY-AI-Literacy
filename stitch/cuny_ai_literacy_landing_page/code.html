Bridging Learning with Industry

The CUNY AI Literacy and Professional Readiness Series is a Spring 2026 initiative focused on helping students develop practical and responsible AI skills.

The program brings together students from across CUNY and NYU through hands-on, industry-informed sessions that connect classroom learning with real-world applications in research, business, and career development.
